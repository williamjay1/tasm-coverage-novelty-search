2026-09-29. Status: executable audit plan, **not audit results**. Scope is the existing public patent data. No expert recruitment or simulated human ratings are required. ## Claim boundary

The study measures grant-visible shortlists, name-based patent portfolios, classification unfamiliarity and external technical attention. These are not filing-visible information, verified corporate groups, absorptive capacity, cognitive distance, adoption, commercial value or observed staff time. The alternative title quartile measures relative distance within an eligible pool, not absolute novelty.

## Reconciliation with the current implementation

- The evaluator implements historical training, common pools, quota floors, MMR and development-only rules; implementation does not establish that real-data checks passed.
- The measure is a **harmonized CPC/IPC union**, not strict CPC-first fallback, notwithstanding the generic `cpc_codes` field name. The separate title vectorizer now distinguishes distance from title-and-abstract prediction.
- Full-owner citation exclusion and exact windows require upstream edge-level verification before completed-tense claims. Current high-attention labels are annual-cohort relative, not within-subfield normalized.
- `num_claims` has a zero fallback but actual claim counts are not established by the selected extraction. Describe absent constant columns as inactive, not measured quality features.
- Retention ratios, zero-denominator handling, category/source sensitivity and temporal summaries require saved results before being described as completed analyses.
- Shared labels exclude candidate owners, not necessarily the focal organization's later citations. Add focal-excluded sensitivity if feasible; otherwise do not claim attention independent of **both** the patent owner and focal organization.
